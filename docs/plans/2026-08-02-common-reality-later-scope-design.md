# Common Reality Later Scope and Product Surface Plan

> **Status:** deferred roadmap; not implemented by Gate 2.

## Purpose

Gate 2 proves a local Packet Studio for creating and reconstructing one
evidence-bound Shared Reality Packet. This document records the later product
surface without quietly treating future designs, adapters, pilots, or theories
as current capability.

The sequencing follows the Common Reality gate contract:

~~~text
Gate 2 Packet Studio
  -> Gate 3 Lens integrity
  -> Gate 4 Bridge responses
  -> Gate 5 Disagreement Map
  -> Gate 6 human pilot
  -> Gate 7 Thought Object memory
  -> Gate 8 AI2AI interoperability
  -> Gate 9 Constellation and forks
  -> Gate 10 Chorus federation
~~~

Every later gate remains local-first and typed. Models may propose candidate
text or objects, but deterministic validators, grants, receipts, and human
decisions remain authoritative.

## Gate 3 — Lens integrity

### Deliver

- declared presentation profiles;
- an allowlisted transformation grammar;
- a Lens compiler and object mapping;
- omission manifests;
- a neutral baseline and at least one alternative Lens;
- a Lens Referee report;
- model/provider swap fixtures that do not change packet authority.

### Proof gate

Across frozen profiles and at least two model families, prove that factual atoms
remain invariant, material counterevidence remains present or is explicitly
reported as omitted, uncertainty does not materially shrink, citations remain
valid, forbidden profile fields are inaccessible, every sentence maps to packet
objects or a labeled inference, and semantic divergence stays inside frozen
tolerances.

### Stop conditions

Stop if a model can introduce an unsupported atom, use a hidden psychological or
political profile, omit material uncertainty without a visible failure, or if a
human reviewer cannot inspect why a presentation was produced.

## Gate 4 — Bridge

### Deliver

- participant packet review;
- accepted and contested claim objects;
- typed dispute categories;
- change conditions;
- action preferences;
- purpose-bound participant export.

### Proof gate

Prove that no private Self Graph object crosses a boundary without an exact
grant, free text cannot silently authorize group action, participants can
correct agent-created perspective objects, and revocation removes an export from
future processing while preserving only a minimal audit receipt.

### Stop conditions

Stop on ambiguous consent, grant scope drift, hidden private-memory export,
automatic commitment of a participant, or any free-text path that bypasses
typed review.

## Gate 5 — Disagreement Map

### Deliver

Classify and display consensus, majority, minority, unresolved questions,
factual conflicts, interpretation conflicts, value conflicts, action conflicts,
missing evidence, and reversible next-action options.

### Proof gate

The minority fixture must survive aggregation; majority status cannot alter
claim status; packet-version mismatch must block aggregation; every map item
must trace to participant packets and packet objects; and no participant may be
represented beyond their grant.

### Stop conditions

Stop if aggregation erases minority evidence, converts agreement into factual
support, merges incompatible packet versions, or produces an untraceable group
summary.

## Gate 6 — Human pilot

### Deliver

- a consented Campus AI Constitution session;
- ordinary-discussion comparison;
- pre/post assessment;
- a human meeting;
- reversible action or explicit no-decision;
- outcome plan and full receipt.

### Proof gate

Pre-register and measure comprehension, uncertainty recall, dispute
localization, opposing-view accuracy, minority retention, agency, trust,
perceived manipulation, willingness to cooperate, and independent mastery. Do
not call the pilot successful because participants agree or enjoy the system.

### Stop conditions

Stop for missing consent, privacy/ethics uncertainty, material participant
harm, pressure to produce a movement claim, missing comparator, or a request to
publish a result before the prespecified analysis.

## Gate 7 — Thought Object memory

Only begin after packet and Lens integrity are proven.

### Deliver

Proposed/accepted/challenged/superseded Thought Objects; encrypted private
source pointers; value, capability, aspiration, tension, boundary, pattern, and
outcome objects; facet export; correction; forgetting; and purpose-bound
retrieval.

### Proof gate

AI inference cannot silently become accepted memory; memory can improve
presentation relevance without changing World Graph content; retrieval exposes
why an object was selected; hidden profile use remains impossible; and forgotten
private source content cannot be recovered through the collective layer.

## Gate 8 — AI2AI interoperability

### Deliver

Optional adapters for A2A agent cards/task lifecycle, MCP packet/evidence
operations, explicit state handles, capability grants, trace propagation, and
model/provider swap.

### Proof gate

Agents collaborate without sharing internals; only typed Bridge/World objects
cross; network/tool actions are policy-authorized; incompatible versions fail
visibly; model swaps preserve packet semantics; and persuasive free text cannot
become authoritative.

Adapters remain optional. The constitutional core must not depend on a
particular provider, protocol, or hosted service.

## Gate 9 — Constellation and forks

### Deliver

Group charter, group agent, Reality Overlap Certificate, canaries, soft fork,
hard fork, merge, and lineage.

### Proof gate

Canary failure freezes consequential action; forks preserve parent lineage and
minority reports; merges require restored overlap and approval; group agents
expire with their charter; and collective memory cannot contain private Self
Graph content.

## Gate 10 — Chorus federation

### Deliver

Group federation, nested maps, cross-group queries, capability matching,
outcome learning, poisoning defense, and witness diversity.

### Proof gate

Subgroup structure remains visible; global averages cannot erase minority
evidence; compromised members or witnesses cannot silently rewrite history;
collective conclusions cite packet versions and group paths; and new groups can
join without exposing personal profiles.

## Cross-gate architecture

The three-graph boundary remains stable:

- **World Graph:** shared packet objects and evidence-bound versions;
- **Self Graph:** private, optional, user-controlled memory;
- **Bridge Graph:** purpose-bound participant responses and exports.

The operation lifecycle extends only after each prerequisite is proven:

~~~text
draft -> source_frozen -> reviewed -> published
  -> lens_generated -> lens_verified
  -> perspectives_collected -> mapped -> deliberated
  -> action_selected -> outcome_observed -> packet_updated
~~~

Every state also needs explicit states named "blocked", "cancelled", "failed",
"exhausted", and "superseded". A later gate may not turn a failed proof into a
success status by changing labels or weakening a frozen fixture.

## Evidence and execution protocol

For every later behavior:

1. add or observe a focused failing fixture;
2. implement the smallest deterministic path;
3. run focused tests and adversarial tests;
4. run aggregate verification;
5. save receipts and artifact paths;
6. update claims only after proof.

Live identity, institution contact, paid infrastructure, sensitive data,
public-pilot claims, destructive changes, and incompatible constitutional
choices are stop conditions requiring a separate decision.

## Explicit non-goals for the deferred plan

This roadmap does not claim sentience, objectivity, neutrality, democratic
legitimacy, model superiority, universal benefit, a real Campus AI policy, or a
successful human pilot. It does not authorize modifying CAM Assistant,
Pendoleum, donor repositories, personal vaults, live corpora, or live
databases.
